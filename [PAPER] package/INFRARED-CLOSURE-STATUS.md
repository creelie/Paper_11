# Infrared proof status

The full four-dimensional string-field-theory infrared problem is not proved in this revision. This status cannot be changed by a successful build of auxiliary lemmas.

Established in ordinary mathematics:

- The stated Poisson emission model has a finite total-energy limit and an explicit Lipschitz-observable cutoff bound (Appendix E).
- Using the credited tree soft theorem and local tree regularity on the specified compact massive kinematics, the subtracted single-real-graviton integral has an O(lambda) tail, with constants displayed (Appendix F).
- Fixed logarithmic growth is integrable after that subtraction, with explicit moments. A regulator-dependent family needs uniform constants and a limit; these are hypotheses of the extension, not proved properties of all string loops.
- An exact two-soft integral has finite separate limits and a divergent joint corner. Its blow-up leaves dr/r. The pure-graviton homogeneous degree count is positive for internal soft interactions under the stated counting assumptions; this does not prove every partial-scaling region convergent.

Missing for the full observable: a specified stable compactification and infrared-safe measurement; a common gauge-compatible real/virtual prescription; a subtraction construction handling every relevant overlapping region and massless species; control of the string-state sums and regulator removal. Appendix F gives a sufficient sector-majorant criterion. It has not been established for the complete string integrand. Perturbative finiteness at each genus would still not be a nonperturbative construction.

The tree soft theorem is credited to Ashoke Sen, JHEP 06 (2017) 113, https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2017)113. The homogeneous gravitational analysis is consistent with Akhoury, Saotome and Sterman, Phys. Rev. D 84 (2011) 104040, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.84.104040. No new false proof by Sen or full infrared resolution is asserted.
