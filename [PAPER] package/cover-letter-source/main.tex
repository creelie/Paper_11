Please consider \emph{Conjectures of Ashoke Sen: Scope, Exact Tests and Analytic Limits}. This is a cover-letter draft for the author's assessment before an actual submission.

The manuscript examines selected duality, tachyon and black-hole claims within their stated frameworks. It reproduces finite negative full dyon indices, corrects the charge-cone transcription, gives a precise effective-dust focusing statement and separates divisor geometry on the F-theory base and orientifold cover. Additional appendices distinguish exact elementary cancellations from a construction of the complete theory.

Known results, Sen's own qualifications and the published cutting-rule erratum remain credited. The paper does not claim a new fatal error in Sen's corrected published proofs, a full nonperturbative construction, or an unconditional resolution of the surveyed physical conjectures. The original 21 coefficient certificates and the larger 30-entry finite scan have different verification scopes, documented in the accompanying source archive.

The author should evaluate whether the independent reproductions and analytical scope results meet the journal's originality threshold. Source code, data, formal-scope reports and a preparation disclosure are included. Required author declarations will be completed in the submission portal after review.
