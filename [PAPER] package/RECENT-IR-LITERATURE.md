# Additional infrared literature check, 26 October 2026

These sources were checked for a possible way to supply the missing full string-amplitude estimate. They are not inserted as hypotheses that silently close the manuscript.

- [Sen, Soft Theorems in Superstring Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.03934), published [JHEP 06 (2017) 113](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2017)113): the relevant tree theorem is established and credited. Appendix F derives a quantitative consequence on the selected massive compact kinematics. This is not a new repair of Sen's proof.
- [Akhoury, Saotome and Sterman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.0270), [Phys. Rev. D 84 (2011) 104040](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.84.104040): gravitational soft and collinear cancellations are analysed at arbitrary fixed order for wide-angle scattering. Their assumptions and sums over diagrams must be retained. This does not by itself provide the full compactified string observable and its other massless sectors.
- [Chicherin, Korchemsky, Sokatchev and Zhiboedov, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23791v2): specific energy correlators have explicit one-loop real/virtual cancellation in gravity and N=8 supergravity; an all-order back-to-back expression and tree-level string applications are presented. The arXiv v2 comment reports JHEP acceptance. A particular asymptotic regime and loop calculation do not give the missing general all-genus string-field remainder estimate. Contact terms also make a distributional formulation relevant.
- [Huang, Wan, Wang and Zhou](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02124v1): the six-point scalar-parity argument removes a regular remainder in a specific tree-level collinear configuration and constrains four-point EFT amplitudes to all orders in their low-energy expansion. Its tree and dispersive hypotheses are explicit. Those EFT orders are not a proof of uniform removal of loop infrared regulators.
- [de Lacroix, Erbin, Kashyap, Sen and Verma](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.06410): the review explicitly retains the infrared divergences of the ordinary amplitudes at D<=4. Its construction and degeneration analysis must not be described as having silently proved the full four-dimensional inclusive limit.

No proof of the full missing estimate was obtained from this check. This is a selected literature examination, not an exhaustive proof that no relevant result exists.
