\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The premise with which this paper began was that the conjectures of Ashoke Sen are for the most part irrelevant, unproved or incomplete. The examination does not bear it out, and it would be a failure of the method to pretend otherwise. Of thirty-six claims, twenty-two are theorems, by the work of Hitchin, Kottke, Rochon, Schnabl, Ellwood, Erler, Maccaferri, Witten, Bringmann, Murthy, Rossell\'o and others and in several cases by Sen's own later papers; ten have passed every test that exists and lack a proof because the framework in which they are made, the path integral on $AdS_2$, the full open string theory, the complete soft expansion, is itself not under control; two were extended beyond the regime in which they were derived and were corrected in the literature; and two are false. The two false claims are not small. The positivity conjecture was the organising principle of a decade of work on the dyon spectrum, and its failure in the toroidal orbifolds, with the hair in every frame that causes it, changes what the conjecture is about: not Fourier coefficients of Siegel forms, but the index of the horizon after the hair is removed, which is the statement of \cite{CD21} and is the one that should be tested in any new model. The identification of tachyon matter with the end of brane decay was the physical picture that the rolling tachyon papers offered, and Theorem~\ref{thm:caustic} shows that the picture is the picture of a set of measure zero in the space of initial conditions; what happens to an inhomogeneous decaying brane after the caustic is one of the open questions that this paper leaves.

The other open questions are, in the order of the catalogue: the $L^2$ cohomology of the monopole moduli spaces for $k\ge3$ beyond the compactly supported part (claim~3); the positivity of the horizon index in $\mathcal N=4$ models with hair in every frame, which is proved for the two toroidal orbifolds and conjectured for none other because no other is known; the existence of a non-perturbative definition of the quantum entropy function (claim~27); a microscopic index for $\mathcal N=2$ black holes (claim~29); a general treatment of the Ramond zero modes in the normalisation of D-instantons (claim~32); and the exponentiation of the infrared divergences behind the quantum soft logarithms (claim~34). To these we add the two questions raised by the failures: the model-independent statement of the positivity conjecture, and the fate of the caustic.

A reader who wants to repeat the computations of this paper will find them in the archive \cite{Bha26}, together with the Lean formalisation of the finite statements: the Fourier coefficients of Tables~\ref{tab:phi10} and~\ref{tab:tor2}, the wall-crossing identities of Theorem~\ref{thm:wallcrossing} and the twenty-one negative indices of Theorem~\ref{thm:positivityfails}, each computed inside the Lean kernel from the definitions of the theta functions. The statements that cannot be formalised, because they are statements about string theory and not about numbers, are the verdicts; for those the paper is the argument.
